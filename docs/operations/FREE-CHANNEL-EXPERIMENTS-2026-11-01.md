3. Community answers: answer genuine relocation questions only where community rules allow links. Give the full useful answer first. Track referrals and completed estimates.
4. Relevant free directories: submit only where tools are accepted and audience is relevant. Track accepted listings and useful referrals; avoid bulk submissions.
5. User-controlled share links: inspect existing calculator share payload for privacy before promotion. Never expose names, addresses, contact details or entered budgets.
6. Editorial outreach: offer a genuinely useful methodology/checklist resource to relevant editors; no invented survey or unsupported market claims.

Measurement:
- Use stable partner-specific UTMs.
- Track sessions, calculator starts, completed estimates, methodology clicks and feedback where analytics supports it.
- Do not put personal details or entered cost values into URLs or analytics.
- Evaluate a 30-day pilot against a baseline; small samples are directional only.
- Stop a channel if it brings irrelevant traffic or complaints.

First low-cost tests: prepare a private embed demo for feedback; test the checklist with a small relevant audience; run one community answer only after checking that links are allowed.
