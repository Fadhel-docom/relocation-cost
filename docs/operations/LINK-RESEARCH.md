## Prioritization and safeguards

1. **Do not send outreach yet.** This report is a research shortlist, not permission to contact anyone.
2. The strongest plausible non-competitor lead is NerdWallet by topic, but it has a high editorial bar and no evidence that it accepts external tool submissions. The university pages are mostly niche-specific; relevance is not enough to justify a link request.
3. Avoid paid links, link exchanges, mass email, automated submissions, fake accounts, and requests to insert links into unrelated pages.
4. The best long-term asset for earning legitimate citations is a distinct resource worth referencing: a transparent, source-backed estimate methodology, clear assumptions, and useful results without a lead form. Do not promise savings or accuracy beyond evidence.
5. Before any outreach, verify the destination page is live, read its current editorial/external-link policy, identify the responsible editor, and tailor a short message. If no clear fit/policy exists, skip it.
