## FMCSA sources reviewed
The official FMCSA pages support the distinction between a planning estimate from this tool and a mover's written estimate for an interstate shipment. They also distinguish binding from non-binding estimates. The 110% delivery rule relates to certain non-binding interstate estimates; it is not the calculator's 10% planning contingency.

Sources:
- https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/consumer-protection/protect-your-move/what-binding-move-estimate
- https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/how-to/subpartD
