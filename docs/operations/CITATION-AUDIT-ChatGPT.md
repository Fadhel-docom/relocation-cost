# Independent citation audit — three Relocation Cost guides

**Reviewer:** ChatGPT  
**Read date:** 2026-10-29 UTC  
**Scope:** Public source pages were opened and compared with the statements in `src/lib/quote-guide.js`, `src/lib/employer-package.js`, and `src/lib/mover-checks.js`. This is a content/source audit, not legal or tax advice. “Confirmed” means the cited page itself was opened and supports the paraphrase; where a source could not be opened, that is stated rather than inferred.

## Findings that need attention

1. **Checking-a-mover guide — 50-mile survey statement is too broad.** The guide says FMCSA requires a physical survey if the reader lives within 50 miles of the mover. The opened FMCSA FAQ says the requirement applies to the *mover transporting the shipment when a broker arranges the move*, if the goods are within a 50-mile radius of the mover or its agent's location, whichever is closer; the consumer may waive it. Revise the sentence to preserve this scope, or cite a source that states the broader rule. Source: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/faq (read 2026-10-29; relevant lines 137–148).
2. **Checking-a-mover guide — FTC-backed claims remain partly unverified in this audit.** The FTC page timed out when opened. Search-result excerpts are not enough to mark its claims “confirmed.” The FTC rows below remain partial/unverified until the page itself can be read.
3. **Employer guide — Form 3903 page-update date.** The 2025 instructions and military exception were confirmed. The page itself identifies the document as “Instructions for Form 3903 (2025)”; the separate “April 30, 2026” update date was not visible in the opened page text during this audit. Keep that date only if a directly visible official page field confirms it.
