# Awin application draft

Relocation Cost is an independent US moving-cost planning tool built to help people prepare relocation budgets before requesting provider quotes.

The site provides a free calculator based on distance, home size, move type, and user-entered relocation expenses. It also publishes state-to-state route guides and methodology notes explaining which figures are sourced and which are explicit planning assumptions.

Our editorial approach avoids presenting estimates as quotes or guaranteed prices. Published market figures are identified as source data, assumptions are labeled, and the disclosure explains that the site currently has no affiliate links or advertising. Future commercial relationships will be disclosed and will not determine the calculator methodology or estimates.

We are applying to Awin to add relevant moving-related partners only after approval, with clear disclosure for visitors.
